Muhammad Adnan, Ph.D.

Research Professor, Graduate School of Energy Science and Technology (GEST), Chungnam National University

Muhammad Adnan’s research explores the physics, chemistry, and materials science of advanced semiconductors and optoelectronic devices, spanning both solution- and vacuum-deposition approaches. He focuses on developing and understanding organic, organic-inorganic, and all-inorganic semiconductor materials, nanoparticles, and emerging metal-halide perovskites for next-generation photovoltaics/optoelectronic technologies.

Focus areas

  • Thermally evaporated perovskites

    Fully vacuum-processed, all-inorganic perovskite solar cells, including top cells for tandem photovoltaics.

  • Interfaces and passivation

    Identifying and mitigating electronic, interfacial, and operational losses that limit efficiency and long-term stability.

  • Ultrafast spectroscopy

    Photoinduced and transient spectroscopies that reveal charge generation, transport, recombination, and energy transfer.

  • Semiconductor materials

    Organic, organic-inorganic, and all-inorganic semiconductors, nanoparticles, and functional composites.
